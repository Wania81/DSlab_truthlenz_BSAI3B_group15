TruthLens

AI-Based News Credibility Analyzer

Problem

Fake and misleading news spreads fast and readers cannot easily judge what to trust. TruthLens scores articles using clear credibility indicators and flags suspicious ones.

What it does

  • Stores and indexes news articles
  • Scores credibility (0–100)
  • Finds related stories
  • Flags misleading content
  • Lists the most suspicious articles

How it works

Data structures used

StructureRole in TruthLens
Hash TableFast article lookup, source reputation, keyword index
Linked ListHash chaining, triggered indicators, analysis history
BSTArticles sorted by score, range search
GraphRelated stories (BFS) and clusters (DFS)
Min-HeapTop-K most suspicious articles

Credibility indicators

  • Source reputation
  • Clickbait headline
  • Excess caps / exclamation marks
  • Emotional language
  • Missing author or date
  • No citations
  • Corroboration by related stories
