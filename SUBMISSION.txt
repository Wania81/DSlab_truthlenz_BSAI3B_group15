Project Title: TruthLens - AI-Based News Credibility Analyzer

GitHub Link: https://github.com/Wania81/DSlab_truthlenz_BSAI3B_group15

Excited to share TruthLens, our Data Structures lab project: a news credibility analyzer that scores articles, links related stories, and flags misleading content.

We used Hash Tables for fast lookup and keyword indexing, BSTs for score-ordered storage, Graphs for finding related stories, a Min-Heap for ranking the most suspicious articles, and Linked Lists for chaining and history.

What we learned: choosing the right data structure changes both performance and design, and building each one ourselves made the trade-offs clear. In the era of AI, tools can help with planning, but real understanding comes from implementing the core logic yourself.
